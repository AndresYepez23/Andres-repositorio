1. Privacy in Prisma Space

Prisma Space is designed to execute compatible operations directly in your browser. You do not need to create an account to explore the catalog or use tools that work locally.

Information stored on your device

  • Favorites, recent tools, theme preference, and Prisma Core conversation: stored in local browser storage. Attached files are not stored in the history.
  • The public App Key of Pollinations Connect, if you decide to configure it: stored locally to avoid having to enter it each time.
  • The temporary authorization token of Pollinations: stored in browser session storage and deleted when you end or close that session, or when you disconnect.
  • Personal tokens of Hugging Face, Google Gemini, and Groq: stored in browser session storage while you maintain the connection and deleted when you disconnect or end the session. Not stored in Prisma Core history.

Prisma Space does not currently offer cloud-synced profiles. Favorites and history are not shared between devices.

Analytics and Cookies

The application does not integrate advertising cookies. Prisma records aggregated counters per tool and day: requests, results, failures, duration, and file count, as well as inference attempt counts. These events do not include messages, names, or file content, credentials, or account identifiers. Browser Do Not Track signals are respected. Client-sent counters are indicative and do not identify unique users. The hosting provider may process technical data from requests to operate and protect the service, in accordance with their own terms.

2. Files, Processing, and External Providers

PWA Installation

Prisma Space installation as an application is optional. The service worker stores static files to improve startup and some offline functions. It does not cache private API responses, message content, or authentication keys.

Optional Accounts and Synchronization

Prisma can be used without registering. The account system in the independent Prisma Space project will be enabled when its infrastructure is verified. If you decide to register and explicitly authorize synchronization, the selected history text will be stored to recover it from other devices. Attached files or downloadable results will not be automatically uploaded. Closing the session deletes the synchronized local copy on that device; remote deletion is managed under the authenticated account.

Voice Entry

Voice dictation may use browser recognition or Groq transcription through the Prisma server when the browser does not support it. In the latter case, the audio recording is sent to the provider only after pressing the microphone. The current spoken conversation works in turns, not as continuous audio calls.

Local Processing

When a tool indicates that it works locally or in the browser, the file is processed on your device, and Prisma Space does not receive it on a server of its own. Performance depends on the browser, memory, and equipment you use.

Prisma Core and External Processing

Prisma Core sends the text of your message and the recent conversation context to the connected text model to prepare a plan. Only basic metadata of attached files (name, type, and size) are shared with the model; the content is not automatically attached for visual analysis. Afterward, selected tools process files according to their mode: local ones keep them on the device, and external ones transmit necessary data to the corresponding provider. Prisma requests authorization before executing the plan.

Other functions need to communicate with third-party services. In those cases, the necessary data to complete the operation is sent to the selected provider:

  • Pollinations Connect: may receive text prompts, generation requests, text-to-speech, or audio files for transcription, depending on the tool used.
  • Hugging Face Inference Providers: receives prompts, images, or other necessary data according to the model and tool authorized with your token.
  • Groq and Google Gemini: may receive the text of your requests and work context when you choose them as text or multimodal engines.
  • Frankfurter API: receives the necessary currency codes to consult reference rates.
  • Processing libraries and fonts: some dependencies are downloaded from CDN services such as esm.sh, unpkg, cdnjs, and Google Fonts.

The treatment made by these providers is also governed by their own policies, conditions, limits, and availability. Prisma Space does not control their systems and cannot guarantee their practices.

Recommendation: do not load medical, financial, employment, personal, or confidential information into tools that send content to external providers, unless you have reviewed their conditions and have authorization to do so.

Deletion and control

You can delete favorites, history, theme, and the App Key local from your personal space menu. You can also disconnect Pollinations from the AI Laboratory. Deleting local data does not remove records that an external provider has already received.
