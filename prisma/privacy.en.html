1. Data, Accounts and Storage

You can browse Prisma Space and use compatible tools without creating an account. Some features require an external connection or an account to save preferences and synchronize conversations.

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Depending on the sign-in method, this may involve your email address, account identifier, and basic information supplied by the sign-in provider, such as Google. Prisma Space does not store passwords in plain text. Your Prisma profile may store a display name, theme, and synchronization setting.

Optional conversation synchronization

Conversation history stays on your device by default. If you sign in and explicitly confirm that you want synchronization, the text of your conversations and related metadata —such as title, order, date, and pinned status— are stored in your Supabase account so they can be restored on other devices. Attachments, File objects, and downloadable files are not synchronized with conversation history. You can delete conversations from the history or turn synchronization off.

Information stored on your device

  • Favorites, recently used tools, theme preferences, and local history: browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB).
  • Personal Hugging Face, Google Gemini, and Groq keys: browser session storage (sessionStorage); removed when disconnected or when the session ends.
  • The public Pollinations Connect App Key, if you choose to configure it, may be kept locally. Temporary authorization tokens are stored in session storage.
  • The PWA may cache static resources to improve loading or offline behavior. Private API responses and authentication keys are not stored in that cache.

Transactional emails

After a new account is confirmed, Prisma Space may send a non-promotional welcome email to the verified address. Delivery is handled by Resend, and its technical status is recorded to prevent duplicates.

2. Files, AI and External Providers

Local and external processing

Tools identified as local process files on your device. Some features run in the browser and may download required libraries or models. When you choose a connected tool or authorize an external operation, the information needed to complete it is sent to the relevant provider.

Prisma Core

To prepare a response, Prisma Core sends your message text and recent conversation context to the selected or available text model. Attachments are not automatically sent to the model that plans the task: the planner may receive basic metadata such as file name, type, and size. If you then authorize a tool that must analyze or transform file content through an external service, the necessary content is sent to that provider. Local operations keep the file on your device.

Providers that may be involved

Depending on the tool, selected model, and availability, text, image, audio, or video processing may involve Google Gemini, Hugging Face, Groq, Pollinations, OpenRouter, Kilo, Requesty, SenseNova, Z.AI/Zhipu, AIHubMix, or Cloudflare Workers AI. Web research queries may be sent to Tavily or Jina AI. Currency conversion may query Frankfurter. These services are not all involved in every request.

These providers receive only the information needed for the feature you request, which may include prompts, context, images, audio, or documents when the operation requires them. Each provider has its own terms, retention practices, and privacy policy; review them before submitting confidential information.

Infrastructure and dependencies

Hosting, server functions, usage limits, and some abuse protection use Cloudflare. Authentication and optional synchronization use Supabase. Transactional email uses Resend. Some libraries, models, resources, or fonts may be downloaded from CDNs such as esm.sh, unpkg, cdnjs, and Google Fonts; those services may receive technical connection data, such as your IP address and browser information, when requested.

Recommendation: avoid entering medical, financial, employment, personal, or confidential information into external services unless you have reviewed their terms and are authorized to share it.

3. Cookies, Advertising and Google AdSense

Prisma Space does not currently display ads and has not activated Google AdSense advertising integration. The application uses local and session browser storage to remember preferences, sessions, and connections; these mechanisms are not, by themselves, advertising cookies.

If Google AdSense is activated, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other browser or device identifiers to serve ads, limit their frequency, measure performance, and, depending on configuration and applicable consent, personalize ads based on previous visits to this or other websites.

External vendors and advertising networks involved in ad delivery may place and read cookies in your browser or use similar technologies. If networks other than Google are added, this policy will be updated to identify them and link to their privacy choices.

You can manage Google ad personalization through Google Ads Settings or review opt-out options for some vendors at aboutads.info. You can also read how Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps.

Where ads are served to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Prisma Space will configure a Google-certified consent management platform when required for compliance with rules on personalized advertising.

4. Metrics, Security and Retention

When application telemetry is enabled, Prisma records aggregate counters by day and tool, such as event type, duration, and file count. These events do not include prompts, messages, file names, document content, credentials, or account identifiers.

To enforce usage limits and prevent abuse, the server may use the IP address received from Cloudflare infrastructure and store a hash-derived fingerprint in its counters instead of the untransformed IP address. Cloudflare may also process IP addresses, headers, and technical metadata to provide and protect hosting, under its own terms.

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or clear browser data. Personal keys saved in sessionStorage are removed when the session ends or when you disconnect them. Synchronized conversation data remains in your account until you delete the conversations or the relevant account is handled. External providers apply their own retention periods.

To report a problem or request a private channel for a personal-data inquiry, visit About Prisma Space and Support. Do not post passwords, tokens, email addresses, conversations, or documents in public reports.

  • External providers may apply their own quotas, restrictions, or prices. Check their terms before authorizing usage.
  • Keep a copy of your original files and verify results before relying on them. Prisma Space does not guarantee uninterrupted availability or fitness for a particular purpose.
