1. What Prisma Space is

Prisma Space is an independent web platform that combines utilities for documents, images, audio, video, text, data, study, and creative work with Prisma Core, an assistant that helps interpret requests and coordinate compatible tools.

The goal is to help people move from a concrete task to an appropriate tool without having to navigate unrelated services. The catalogue and information pages are organized by function, category, and workflow.

2. How the tools work

  • Local processing: the operation runs on your device and the file does not need to be sent to a Prisma server.
  • Browser processing: the feature runs in your browser and may download libraries or models needed to complete the task.
  • Connected service: the feature must communicate with an external provider. The information needed for the task is transmitted when you choose and authorize that operation.

Each tool page describes its purpose, main steps, supported formats, and known limitations. Features that are still being developed are marked Beta or are not presented as available.

3. Quality, privacy and limitations

Instructions and information pages aim to explain what each tool does and what it does not do. Not all features have the same availability: some run locally, others depend on external models or providers, and some remain in Beta.

AI results may be inaccurate or incomplete. Check documents, images, text, and other outputs before using them. Always keep a copy of the original file when the result matters.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before processing personal or confidential information. Connected features may be subject to third-party terms and limits.

4. Support and reports

For general inquiries, requests about your personal data, or private contact, email proyectosmetas25@gmail.com.

To report technical issues, provide reproduction steps, or suggest improvements, use the project's public GitHub issue channel.

Protect your information: GitHub reports may be public. Do not include passwords, API keys, tokens, private conversations, or personal files. For requests about your data, use the contact email listed above.

Prisma Space does not use public reports to request credentials or private files.
